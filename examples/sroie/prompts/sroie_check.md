Check four fields that an earlier step extracted from a receipt. You get the receipt and the fields.

- company: the seller's company name, copied as printed.
- date: the receipt date as DD/MM/YYYY.
- address: the seller's address on one line, copied as printed.
- total: the amount paid, two decimals, no currency.

Keep a field when the receipt confirms it and fix it when the receipt shows another value. Return an empty string for a field the receipt does not show.
